## Identity

- **Name:** Syndy Emade Foundation / Fondation Syndy Emade (Cameroon)
- **Tagline (from the official logo):** "Together we can" — FR: « Ensemble, nous pouvons »
- **Founder:** Syndy Emade (Elone Synthia Emade), award-winning Cameroonian actress, producer and CEO of Blue Rain Entertainment
- **Active since:** at least August 2019 (launch video on SYNDY EMADE TV, 18 Aug 2019)
- **Base:** Douala (most documented outreach is in Douala, especially Bonaberi)
- **Stated cause (founder, in the Christmas outreach video):** fighting the exploitation of street children ("contre l'exploitation des enfants de la rue")
- **Approach (founder, Dec 2023):** the team visits homes, often in remote areas, and the founder then visits in person to decide who needs help most

## Programmes, with documented activity

### 1. Back to School — education support
- Donated school materials to **over 600 internally displaced children in Douala**. Sources: Positive Vibes CMR / J Martins Promo / Ekwat TV Facebook posts.
- Paid **school fees for 300 children**. Source: Positive Vibes CMR Facebook post ("supports 300 children with school fees").
- Ran a **Back-to-School Campaign** to give underprivileged children the basics they need for school. Source: Facebook post quoting the Foundation.
- *Planned (from the Foundation's own brief):* secondary and university scholarships, and vocational and digital-skills training.

### 2. Support for displaced families (IDPs)
- Visited families displaced from the **North-West and South-West regions** now living in **Bonaberi, Douala**, providing food and essential supplies. Sources: Kesa Magazine Facebook post, Newsday Cameroon, YouTube video "Syndy Emade touching moments with IDPs" (2 Feb 2021).
- In the video, displaced families and a host describe the gift as feeding their children for several days, with some planning to share it with neighbours and relatives in the village.
- The outreach facilitator said its aim was "bringing hope to those in hopeless situations", and the founder called it "the first of many" (Newsday Cameroon).
- The mobile crisis relief work is named **"Chantiers Mobiles Crise"** (mentioned in the Christmas outreach video).

### 3. Christmas outreach — vulnerable families
- **Christmas outreach to families at Bonaberi** (video published 8 Jan 2021).
- **Christmas 2023:** a single mother who is visually impaired, living in one cramped room with her three sons. The Foundation paid **one year's rent** for a larger home, **pledged to renovate** their room, and delivered rice, cooking oil, water, soap and other household essentials. Source: Mimi Mefo Info, 27 Dec 2023.

### 4. Child protection — street children
- Stated mission: protecting street children from exploitation (founder in the Christmas outreach video).
- *Planned (from the Foundation's own brief):* shelter, nutrition, health check-ups, mentoring.

### 5. Women & partnerships
- Marked **International Women's Day in partnership with the Wilmbi Foundation** (Facebook post by the Foundation, reshared). *To confirm:* date and details.
- *Planned (from the Foundation's own brief):* micro-grants and training in tailoring and agriculture for widows and displaced women.
